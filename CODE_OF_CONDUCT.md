# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FIRBO AI a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and precise. Assume good intent, disagree with ideas rather than people, protect private information, and avoid abusive, discriminatory, threatening, or harassing conduct.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, sexualized conduct, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential sharing, malicious code or dependency submissions, and other conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Security vulnerabilities or accidentally exposed credentials should be reported privately to the repository owner as described in `SECURITY.md`; do not post them in public issues.

## Scope

This policy applies in FIRBO AI project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
